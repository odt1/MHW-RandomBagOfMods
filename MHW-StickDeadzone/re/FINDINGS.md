# MHW Stick Deadzone: reverse-engineering findings

Every claim is marked **observed** (seen in disassembly, decompiled code, memory or a live trace), **inferred** (concluded from observations) or **unknown**. Binary: `MonsterHunterWorld.exe`, packed SHA-256 `C2EBBBD2C49F216D484E31A5219BED419EB1E5E7D206D02CBA040A3AB79D90EA` (the build FairTransmog and Auto-Auto-Sort support). Addresses are RVAs in the unpacked image (`C:\Projects\MHW Mods\re\inputs\mhw-current\MonsterHunterWorld.exe.unpacked.exe`, BN database beside it). Live results are from the user's game on 2026-10-22/23 with an XInput pad with TMR sticks. Class names come from the game's DTI (`tools/bn/run_dti.sh`, `tools/live/singles.js`).

## Input path, pad to floats

- **Observed:** the pad singleton is `sMhSteamController` (global pointer `0x51C4558`). It holds 4 devices of 0x2D8 bytes at `+0x38`; the selected index is at `+0x1518`.
- **Observed:** `sub_1422A59D0` polls one device: type 0 is a Steam Controller (`sub_1422A5710`), type 2 DirectInput (`GetDeviceState` of 0x110 bytes, then `sub_1422A0880`), anything else XInput (`XInputGetState` from `XINPUT9_1_0.dll`, then `sub_1422A1280`). Live: type 4, XInput index 0.
- **Observed:** `sub_1422A1280` stores the raw thumbs as int32 in the device: RX `+0x178`, RY `+0x17C`, LX `+0x180`, LY `+0x184`, triggers `+0x188/+0x189`. Stick-as-button bits, which menus navigate with, use the int16 threshold at device `+0x1A` (live 16383, about 0.5). **Inferred:** the DirectInput and Steam paths fill the same fields, since everything after this reads only the device.
- **Observed:** the pad singleton's update `sub_141B168C0` is reached through `sMhSteamController`'s vtable `0x2F87138` (RVA, as all vtables here), index 6 (slot RVA `0x2F87168`); `sMhPad`'s vtable `0x33FC580` also holds it. It calls `sub_1418D7720(padinfo, device)` (its only caller, `+0x1B1694A`), which converts each axis with `sub_142107CB0(raw, 0.0)`: `raw/32767` or `raw/32768`, with a per-axis deadzone argument that is always `0.0` (`xorps xmm1, xmm1` before each of the 4 calls). Output at singleton `+0xC28`: LX `+0x18`, LY `+0x1C`, RX `+0x20`, RY `+0x24`, left length `+0x28`, right length `+0x2C`. The floats have **no deadzone** at this point.
- **Observed:** `sub_1418D7C40` copies that 0xB8-byte block into two contexts, `+0xCE0` (context 0) and `+0xD98` (context 1). Afterwards `sub_141B17080` overwrites context 0's sticks from keyboard keys (`sMhKeyboard`, `0x51C53B0`, `+0x27A8..+0x27C4`) and the mouse (`sMhMouse`, `0x51C53F0`, `+0x1548`) while they are used.
- **Observed:** the stick accessors are `sub_141B15DA0` (LX), `0x1B15E70` (LY), `0x1B160E0` (RX), `0x1B161B0` (RY), `0x1B15C90` (left length), `0x1B15FD0` (right length), with the context as argument 2. A live Interceptor trace in the hub (4 s) saw one caller: `sub_1411A9C50` (`+0x11AA1AF..+0x11AA1E7`), once per frame, context 0.
- **Observed:** `sub_1411A9C50` copies LX, LY, RX, RY unchanged to the hunter's controller, `cHumanControllerPlMaster` (vtable `0x322BBB0`), at `+0x30/+0x34/+0x38/+0x3C`. Its `+0xDB0` is the `uPlayer`.

## The deadzones

All are plain cuts: a value under the threshold counts as 0, a value at or over it is used unchanged. Nothing rescales after the cut.

- **Observed:** `rPlayerParam` (global pointer `0x5011F58`, 2841 code references; **inferred** to be loaded from `common\pl\pl_param.plp`) live values: `+0x120` 0.60, `+0x124` 0.30, `+0x128` 0.25, `+0x12C` 0.15, `+0x130` 0.15, `+0x134` 0.30, `+0x138` 0.85, `+0x13C` 0.30, `+0x150` 0.70, `+0x154` 0.20, `+0x158` 90.
- **Observed:** `cHumanControllerPad` (vtable `0x322B910`, update `sub_141230BB0` at slot 6) copies the hunter's sticks to `+0x968/+0x96C` (left) and `+0x990/+0x994` (right) at `+0x1231046..+0x1231067`. Its constructor `sub_14122E8C0` places two stick helpers whose update it calls through slot `+0x28`: `+0x950`, vtable `0x32418D0`, update `sub_14122DB00`, sets its flag `+0x958` while the left stick's length is at least `rPlayerParam+0x124`; `+0x978`, vtable `0x3241900`, update `sub_14122DE20`, sets `+0x980` while the right stick's length is at least `+0x128`.
- **Observed, movement:** the left helper's flag is read through `sub_14122FF80` (61 callers) and directly in `sub_141230BB0` (`*(arg1 + 0x958) == 0`). The same update also compares the left length `+0x960` with `sub_1419A0800(0)`, which returns `rPlayerParam+0x134` (`+0x12316BC`, `+0x1231942`, `+0x1232C6B`); `sub_1419A0800(1)` returns `+0x138`, `(2)` returns `+0x13C`. **Inferred:** the hunter starts walking once the left stick's length reaches 0.30. **Unknown:** what `+0x138` (0.85) and `+0x13C` decide; probably walk and run.
- **Observed, main camera, gate:** `sub_14122FFA0` (16 callers, among them the camera update `sub_1412A0530` at `+0x12A22E5` and its stick handler `sub_1412A2A30` at `+0x12A2CE3`, `+0x12A2F2E`, `+0x12A302C`) returns true while the mouse moves or while the right helper's flag `+0x980` is set. **Inferred**, and confirmed by the live tests below: the camera turns only while the right stick's length is at least 0.25.
- **Observed, main camera, per axis:** `sub_1412A0530` reads the right stick through `sub_14122FF90` (`return pad + 0x990`). `sub_1412A9B70` copies `rPlayerParam+0x12C/+0x130` into the camera's block at `+0x1578/+0x157C` every frame (live: 251 calls in 2 s, as many as the next function). `sub_141F9EA40` zeroes the camera input `+0xA90` / `+0xA94` when its absolute value is under `+0x1578` / `+0x157C`, then passes each axis through `sub_142176600(v, 2)`, a signed integer power (a loop of multiplications, exponent 2 passed as an immediate), then multiplies by the camera's speed settings and an acceleration ramp (`+0xA98/+0xA9C`, from `+0x638/+0x63C` toward `+0x640/+0x644`). **Inferred:** the camera turns at `sign(v)·v²` of each axis past 0.15, so its slowest real turn on an axis, at the 0.25 gate, is 6.25% of full speed.
- **Observed, other readers:** inline per-axis `0.3` cuts, rescaled as `(|v|-0.3)/0.7` or `(|v|-0.3)*k`, in `sub_1404DA030` (LX/LY), `sub_1405374B0` (RX/RY, a mode-dependent camera) and `sub_1410D5AE0` (`uGuildCardCamera`); round `0.25` cuts in `sub_14126C8C0` (right length) and `sub_140582820` (`x²+y² >= 0.0625`). `sub_14122DBA0` is a left-stick flick detector (`+0x150/+0x154/+0x158`). **Unknown:** which screens use each; none of them is the hunter's normal movement or camera.

## What the plugin does with this

- **Inferred** from the cuts and the user's tests: "as before, just without the deadzone" means mapping the stick's travel past the user's deadzone onto the part of the range the game reacts to, as one radius, and leaving every game threshold and curve alone. Left stick onto 0.30..1 (`max(+0x124, +0x134)`), right stick onto 0.25..1 (`max(+0x128, +0x12C, +0x130)`). The first touch then gives the game's slowest walk or camera turn, full tilt stays full tilt, and a small sideways part of a mostly straight push stays under the camera's 0.15 axis cut, as in the unmodded game.

## Live test history

- **Observed (2026-10-23 ~00:00):** a Frida prototype that inflated the floats at the source to beat an assumed deadzone of 0.30 (right stick per axis) felt wrong: "Scaling feels way off, e.g. camera is too sensitive. It should be linear and behave as before, just without the deadzone (in other words - give more dynamic range)." **Inferred:** the assumption was too high for the camera's axis cut, and inflating each axis turned noise on the other axis into a fast diagonal turn.
- **Observed (~00:20):** writing `rPlayerParam+0x12C/+0x130` = 0.03 and `+0x134` = 0.05 into memory held, but "Didn't seem to work in-game". **Inferred:** movement is gated by `+0x124`, not `+0x134`; the camera by `+0x128` before its axis cuts, and the squared curve makes speeds under the old cut invisible anyway. The user asked for 0.01 as the testing default from then on.
- **Observed (01:00, plugin 0.1.0):** left stick radial onto 0.30, right stick per axis onto 0.15: "Much better now". Left: wants a deadzone of 0.05, then reacts to the slightest touch. Right: "still needs >0.05 to start moving even with 0 deadzone set."
- **Observed (01:00-01:20):** right stick variants. Response=Linear (axis `sqrt(0.0225 + 0.9775·travel)`): "Now it triggers correctly, but the response itself feels off. Game was perfect by feel." MinSpeed=0.05 (axis `0.224 + 0.776·travel`): "I can stay at 0.03 for hours, and the camera won't move." **Inferred:** a 0.03 tilt mapped to 0.247 and 0.227, both under the 0.25 gate; Linear passed it from 0.034. This exposed the gate.
- **Observed (after the 01:19 deploy, commit `c83cb3c`):** right stick radial onto 0.25: "Finally, a real progress."

## Tooling incidents

- **Observed:** a Frida hardware-watchpoint probe (`Thread.setHardwareWatchpoint`) crashed the game at 2026-10-22 23:52:37 with exception `0x80000004` (single step) at `+0x1B15E68`, minutes after the script had unset the watchpoints and detached. **Inferred:** a debug register stayed armed on one thread. Do not use Frida hardware watchpoints in this game.
- **Observed:** the game crashed at 2026-10-23 00:31:30 inside `frida-agent.dll` (exception `0xC0000409`), during or right after a read-only script, in a process that had seen about ten Frida sessions, one of them (with an Interceptor hook) ended by killing the CLI instead of detaching. **Inferred:** the agent was left in a bad state. Keep Frida sessions in one game process few, let `-t` end them, and test through the plugin's own `[Debug] LogSticks=1` instead.
